Bhumi App – Bihar Land Info

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bhumi App – Bihar Land Info ("we", "us", "the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Android application and related website (together, the "Service"). By using the Service you agree to the practices described here.

1. What the App does

Bhumi App helps you reach Bihar land-record services. It shows the official "View Jamabandi (Panji-II)" search, opens other Government of Bihar land services inside the App, and shows land, scheme and Bihar news headlines. This is an independent app. It is not an official app of the Government of Bihar and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government body. The App does not create, change or approve any government record.

2. Information We Collect

  • Land-record search inputs you type (district, circle, halka, mauja, khata / khesra / raiyat name / jamabandi number, page numbers and the security-code answer). They are sent directly to the official Bihar Bhumi portal to get your result. We do not keep them on our servers.
  • No accounts: we do not ask you to register, and we do not collect your name, phone number, address, Aadhaar, or payment information.
  • Device and usage data collected by our advertising partners (see Section 5): advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data.
  • Log and analytics data on our website (IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps) for security and aggregate statistics.

3. Information stored only on your device

The App saves the following on your phone. It is not sent to us:

  • Your last selected village (district / circle / halka / mauja) so the next search starts faster.
  • Your recent searches and the services you mark as saved (♥).
  • Your language choice and notification settings.
  • The latest news headlines, kept briefly so they appear quickly and refreshed every 3 hours.

Jamabandi registers themselves are fetched fresh from the official portal each time and are not cached by the App. You can clear all of the above by clearing the App's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the App.

4. Features that use your device

  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, the App checks Google News about every 3 hours (even when closed) and may notify you about new land, scheme and Bihar news. You can turn this off in the App (Notifications → settings) or in Android settings. Quiet hours (10 pm – 7 am) are on by default.
  • Downloads: when you tap PDF, PNG or Excel (CSV) the file is saved to your Downloads folder (Downloads/BiharBhumi) or shared by you through Android's share sheet. The App does not read other files on your device.
  • File upload: if a government form inside the App asks you to attach a document, Android's own file picker opens and only the file you choose is given to that form.
  • Internet access: needed to reach the official portals, news and ads.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network)

The App shows ads served through Google AdMob, with Meta Audience Network taking part in AdMob mediation (bidding). The ad formats are banner, interstitial, rewarded and app-open ads.

  • These partners may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad-interaction data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. They may use this to personalize ads.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads (path varies by phone). You can also visit Google Ads Settings and Meta ad preferences.
  • See Google's How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Privacy Policy, and Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Our ads.txt file lists the ad systems authorized to sell our inventory.
  • Our website, if it shows ads, may also use Google AdSense cookies; opt out at www.aboutads.info.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. Data goes to:

  • Official government portals (such as emutation.bihar.gov.in and biharbhumi.bihar.gov.in): the search details you enter are sent to them to retrieve records.
  • Google News: the App requests public news feeds from Google News. Opening a headline loads the publisher's page.
  • Advertising partners listed in Section 5.
  • Hosting and analytics providers for our website (for example Vercel and GitHub Pages).
  • Legal requirements, when disclosure is required by law or valid legal process.

7. Government and third-party pages inside the App

Services such as mutation, LPC, land rent, Bhu-Naksha, Parimarjan Plus and court services open inside the App in a web view but run on the government's own websites. Anything you type there (including a citizen login) goes to that government website, not to us, and their privacy policies apply. We cannot see or store what you enter on those pages. The same applies to news articles opened from headlines.

8. Data Retention and Security

  • We keep no search or land-record data on our own servers.
  • Data stored on your device stays there until you clear it or uninstall the App.
  • Website logs are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting.
  • We use reasonable measures (HTTPS connections, minimal collection) to protect data, but no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, withdraw consent, and complain to a supervisory authority. Because we collect almost no personal data ourselves, most choices are in your hands: turn off notifications, reset your advertising ID, clear the App's storage, or uninstall. For anything else, write to us below.
