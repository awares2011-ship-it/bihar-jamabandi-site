This Privacy Policy explains how Bhumi App ("we", "us", "the App/Website") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website, Android application, and related services (together, the "Service"). By using the Service you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

  • Search inputs you type: district, circle, halka, mauja, khesra/mutation details and similar land-record filters you use in the live Jamabandi search. These are processed only to produce your result.
  • Log data: IP address, browser/device type, pages visited, and timestamps, collected automatically by our host and analytics provider for security, debugging, and aggregate traffic statistics.
  • No accounts: we do not ask you to register, and we do not collect your name, phone number, address, Aadhaar, or payment information through the Service.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: used for essential operation, preferences, and advertising (see Section 4).

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Service.
  • To fetch and display the land-record information you request.
  • To monitor aggregate usage, fix errors, and improve performance.
  • To detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, or security issues.
  • To display advertising and measure ad performance.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

  • Service providers who host the site, deliver analytics, or serve ads (for example Vercel and Google) — strictly for the purposes described in this policy.
  • Official government portals (such as biharbhumi.bihar.gov.in): your search parameters are sent to these public services to retrieve records. Their own privacy policies apply to their systems.
  • Legal requirements, when disclosure is required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.

4. Advertising and Cookies (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our Service and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies at www.aboutads.info.
  • For more information, see Google's Advertising Technology Policy and Privacy Policy.
  • Our ads.txt file lists authorized sellers for our inventory.

5. Analytics

We use privacy-focused, aggregated analytics (Vercel Analytics) to understand general traffic patterns. These tools do not build cross-site profiles of individual users and do not use advertising cookies.

6. Third-Party Links and Content

The Service links to official government sites, news feeds, and other third-party pages. We are not responsible for the content, accuracy, or privacy practices of those external sites. We encourage you to review their policies.

7. Data Retention and Security

  • Search requests are kept only temporarily (cached briefly to improve speed) and are then deleted.
  • Logs are retained for a limited period needed for security and troubleshooting.
  • We use reasonable technical measures (HTTPS, access controls, minimal collection) to protect data, but no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.
